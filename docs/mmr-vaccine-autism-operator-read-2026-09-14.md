## What changed since the 14 Sep read

* **CHRONOLOGY** (new binding kind): a source cannot support a claim about an event that postdates it. The three GMC / struck-off claims are now withheld — true, but the 1998 paper cannot contain the GMC's 2010 findings. Withholding is honest; a "source confirmed" badge on them was not.
* **The publish gate on already-changed sources:** a claim whose *support* was retracted, corrected, superseded or amended before the freeze is withheld, not published with a warning; only claims *about* such a source pass. On this record that withheld **11 claims** — 9 that rested on the Cochrane pub4 (superseded by pub5) and 2 that rested on Jain 2015 (JAMA erratum). None had another surviving source. **This is the largest single effect of the gate and it is a corpus problem, not an evidence problem:** citing pub5 and reviewing the erratum recovers them. Your call whether "superseded" should count as a refusal for a systematic review that is updated on a cycle.
* The red RETRACTED marker now only ever means "changed after we froze it". At freeze, a retracted paper appears only as the *subject* of a claim, with a grey information note.
