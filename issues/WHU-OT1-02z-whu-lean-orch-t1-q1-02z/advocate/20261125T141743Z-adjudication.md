    MERIT:  yes | partly | no   — and why, citing what you read
            (or not_ruled_protected, only on a protected sentence)

    OMISSION: add | out_of_scope | not_material | not_in_source | not_added_cap

For a QUESTION, from a source only a person is permitted to read:

    ANSWERED BY: a person's name. Not "the team", not a role.
    ON:          the date they opened the document
    ANSWER:      what it says, quoted
    LOCATOR:     section, table or page
    EFFECT:      changes | narrows | sharpens | corrects | none
    DID:         what changed

Or, for a QUESTION the record already answers, a withdrawal:

    WITHDRAWN BY: a person's name, or the role that withdrew it
    ON:           the date
    WHY:          what on the record already settles it
    DID:          what was done instead

### S001-01 — SERIOUS / NARROWS

**Type.** MISSTATES

**We say.** Improving attendance, enrolment and completion was achieved in only one study, which improved teachers' knowledge and attitudes about inclusive education

**They object.** The review also says no included study looked at attendance, enrolment or completion outcomes. What the Kenya study improved was teacher knowledge and attitudes, so the sentence can be read as a measured attendance result that the review does not report.

**Their document says.** No study looked at outcomes related to attendance, enrolment, and completion of people with disabilities in education.
  — *Results summary paragraph on included studies' settings and outcomes (after 'Thirteen interventions were conducted in (inclusive) mainstream settings...')*

**Why it matters.** The review reports no attendance, enrolment or completion outcome from any study. The page's sentence overstates that gap by implying one study achieved those outcomes. The review itself is internally inconsistent here, so a correction should say the evidence on these outcomes is essentially absent.

MERIT:  yes — The review states 'No study looked at outcomes related to attendance, enrolment, and completion,' and the one study described as achieving this goal reported only that 'teacher knowledge and attitudes about inclusive education were improved,' so the sentence should not present attendance, enrolment or completion as achieved; the reader's proposed text was empty, so a corrected text is supplied.
