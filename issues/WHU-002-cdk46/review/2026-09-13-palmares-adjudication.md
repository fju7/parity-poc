## PALM-END-001 — PALMARES-2's endpoint described as the wrong endpoint

**What changed.** Body, S011 note, and the "What all four agree on" summary:
"progression-free survival" → "real-world progression-free survival" at first
use in each; the summary names the statistic ("at an adjusted hazard ratio of
0.91"); the note's opening reads "Real-world progression-free survival,
adjusted hazard ratios:". The open-questions list and the contradiction
paragraph, which said "on both endpoints" / "on progression", now say
real-world progression-free survival.

**Why.** The advisor's precondition on the PALMARES-2 correction (13
September): compare the page's characterisation against S027's verbatim text.
S027: `aHR for rwPFS in the 1L setting was 0.83 (95% CI 0.73-0.95, P = 0.007)
for ribociclib versus palbociclib, 0.76 (95% CI 0.63-0.92, P = 0.004) for
abemaciclib versus palbociclib, and 0.91 (95% CI 0.73-1.14, P = 0.425) for
abemaciclib versus ribociclib.` The page said PFS where S027 says rwPFS in
three places and named no statistic in the summary. Ruled its own entry.

**Bound to.** S027, that span, on every changed sentence carrying the figures.

**Who decided.** The advisor, directive of 13 September 2026 ("PALMARES-2,
two corrections"); applied by Claude Code.

**Effect on this assessment.** The figures are unchanged; what they measure
is now stated correctly, and it is not the trials' endpoint.

**Recorded for readers** in `corrections.md`, 13 September 2026.

**What changed.** Deleted: "It also reports overall survival, pointing the
same way." (body); "on both endpoints" (note and summary); "reports an
exploratory survival result pointing the same way on data its own authors call
immature" and "That was wrong — both programmes reported both endpoints" (the
contradiction paragraph, rewritten to say the page can source the withdrawal
no better than the claim it withdrew); "PALMARES-2's survival data are
immature and its arms unevenly followed" (open questions); "PALMARES-2
publishes no abemaciclib-versus-ribociclib survival figure" (rewritten as a
statement about what we hold); 464, 31.3, 45.7, 25.2, 22.4, "eighteen Italian
centres", and the note's ASCO-2024 figure 0.91 (0.70–1.19). Kept: 1,982 and
the three adjusted hazard ratios, attributed to S027's account. The note now
states that we could not obtain PALMARES-2 (403 to scripts; no browser
connected), what we have instead (S027), that S016 records the 2024
presentation's comparative OS as not reported, that the 2025 publication could
not be checked, and where it was presented and published (S016 ref 19: J Clin
Oncol 2024;42(suppl 16):1014; S027 ref 23: Ann Oncol 2025;36(7):762–774).

**Sweep.** Every sentence naming or implying PALMARES-2 was read (13
September). Beyond the survival sentence and the two "on both endpoints",
five more leaned on its survival result or on the unheld paper's own
descriptions, listed above and all changed. The 1 September change-log
sentence quoting what the page then said is history and stands.

**Why.** The ruling of 13 September: figures from a document we cannot hold
must not be printed as established; a document we hold (S016) records the
presentation as reporting no comparative OS.

**Bound to.** S027 (the aHR span; the reference `Ann Oncol. 2025;36(7):
762–774`), S016 (`did not report comparative OS results (e.g. a multicenter,
real-world, Italian study, PALMARES-2)`; the reference `J Clin Oncol.
2024;42(suppl 16):1014`), S021 (registry fields).

**Who decided.** The advisor, directive of 13 September 2026; applied by
Claude Code.

**Effect on this assessment.** PALMARES-2 now separates palbociclib on one
endpoint on this page, not two; nothing on abemaciclib versus ribociclib
changes.
