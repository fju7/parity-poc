## LIC-Q-001 — three catalogue readings unquoted, the scheme restored

**What changed.** In the S003 corrigendum note, three `<q>` elements — the
Crossref licence URL, Europe PMC's `license: cc by-nc-nd`, and Europe PMC's
`isOpenAccess: N` — are rewritten as unquoted readings taken on 10 September
2026. The URL is printed with its scheme, `http://`, which the quoted form had
dropped. The notice's own footer is named as the held evidence; the catalogues
as corroboration. The disagreement between the two Europe PMC fields is kept.

**Why.** A `<q>` is a quotation, and an elision inside one is the class of
error corrected on 31 August ("A quotation that was not one"). The Crossref
value verified on 2026-09-12 (New York; 2026-09-13 UTC) against the record's own DOI
(10.1016/j.annonc.2025.07.002): `http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/`,
content-version vor, start 2025-07-09. A first fetch against a guessed DOI
returned a different paper's CC-BY licence and was discarded.

**Who decided.** Fred Ugast, operator, 12 September 2026: paraphrase, unquoted;
do not hold two catalogue records to license three field values.

**Effect on this assessment.** None on any figure. The corrigendum's licence and
the page's conclusion about it are unchanged.
