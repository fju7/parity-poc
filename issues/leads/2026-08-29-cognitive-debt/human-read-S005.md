## The questions

Answer whatever you can find and say plainly where you couldn't find an answer.
"Not in the paper" is a real answer and I need it as much as the others.

**1. The corrected effect size.**
Wang & Fan reported g = 0.867 for learning performance. Do these authors give a
corrected figure? Is it about 0.5? Quote the sentence and say whether it is
their own recomputation or something they read off Wang & Fan's own forest plot.

**2. The retracted study.**
Does the paper say Wang & Fan included a study that had itself been retracted?
If so, name it.

**3. How many studies.**
The paper is said to find inconsistency in the *number* of included studies.
What are the conflicting numbers, and where in Wang & Fan does each appear?

**4. Weighting.**
What exactly is the weighting error? One sentence in their words if you can.

**5. Mislabelled studies.**
How many, and mislabelled how — wrong effect direction, wrong sample size,
wrong outcome, attributed to the wrong paper?

**6. Anything about outcome measures.**
Do they say anything about whether the included studies measured learning
*after* ChatGPT was taken away, or performance *with it still available*? This
is the hinge of our piece and I want to know if they touch it.

**7. Scope.**
Do they say these problems are peculiar to Wang & Fan, or common in this
literature? Quote them if they generalise.

**8. The correspondence.**
Do they describe contacting the authors or the journal, and when? The retraction
notice says the authors never responded.
