# Gating checklist — before a word of issue three is drafted

The operator has approved this subject conditional on the case being clear,
convincing, accurate and fair. Everything below is what "accurate and fair"
costs, listed before drafting so that none of it can be discovered afterwards.

---

## THE FAIRNESS POINT THAT GOES NEAR THE TOP OF THE PIECE

**This audit is only possible because Australia published its evidence.**

Most governments do not. Australia produced an Explanatory Memorandum, a formal
Impact Analysis and a Parliamentary Library Bills Digest, put all three in
public, and legislated in the open. Every finding this piece makes exists
because that material can be read.

A piece that treats transparency as a target teaches governments to publish
less, and would deserve to be ignored. So the fairest reading is also the most
convincing one, and it goes early rather than as a closing concession:

- The Impact Analysis **concedes its own evidence gap in its own voice**:
  "while there is a gap in the correlative evidence of the harms of social
  media on young people".
- It **names the failure modes it might encounter** — circumvention, migration
  to unregulated platforms — rather than claiming the measure would work.
- The Bills Digest put the contrary case to legislators **before the vote**,
  including that over 140 researchers thought a ban too blunt.

The criticism is not that the government hid anything. It is that the case, read
against its own sources, is thinner and more repetitive than it appears — and
that the remedy chosen is not the one the sources point to.

- [ ] Counterexample hunter on every universal negative in the draft.
- [ ] Source advocate run with the Australian Government as principal.

## WHAT THE PIECE MAY NOT DO

- Say whether the law is right. Values and rights, not evidence.
- Say whether social media harms teenagers. Unbounded; the National Academies
  2024 report and Rutar's April 2026 comparison both address it better than we
  can, and both get cited rather than re-derived.
- Attribute motive to anyone.
- Present the double-counting as something only a careless government would do.
  We recorded the identical class against ourselves on 29 August, having cited
  P-VERIFY as confirming a claim P-VERIFY had inherited. **The piece says so.**
