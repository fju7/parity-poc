How this works

The short version. The long version is in the policy.

Claims, not takesAssertions are pulled from public sources — peer-reviewed research, regulatory filings, government data — and assessed one at a time.
Scored on six dimensionsSource quality, data support, reproducibility, consensus, recency and rigor. The formula and the anchors are published.
Wrong in publicEvery change to an assessment is logged with its date. When we get one wrong, the correction says what it was responding to.
The disclosure, up front

What Holds Up is published by CivicScale, which sells healthcare software and takes no outside investment. That is a real conflict, not a theoretical one, and the honest thing is to put it where you will see it rather than in a footer.

No customer, commercial partner or subject of an assessment sees one before it publishes. The full position — what money can and cannot buy, and how often our published assessments turn out to be wrong — is in Who Pays for This.
