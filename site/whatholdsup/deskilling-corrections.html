What we corrected on 19 October 2026

On 19 October 2026 we re-read What Happens to the Experts First against its sources and our own records — every sentence above the change history at the foot of the page, the standfirst and the source notes included — and corrected 140 sentences. Each is listed here, grouped by the kind of error, as it stood on the page and as it now reads. Where one sentence was corrected for two reasons it is listed once. The change history itself was not re-read.
