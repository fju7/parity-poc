## Blinding: WHU page furniture (advisor ruling, 2026-10-31)

Round 1's blinding left WHU's scope notes in the text the judges read ("Judged not material to this question",
"Findings in our sources we judged outside this question", "Outside this question", "We did not search / read",
"This answer draws on"). They appear in every WHU answer and in no other system's, and check 3's guessers named them
as their clue (`next_phase/deviations.md`, 2026-10-31).

From round 2: before judging, the blinding step replaces each WHU furniture label with fixed neutral wording, the
same for every answer and **decided and committed before any round-2 answer is captured**. The scope notes' content
stays (it is what WHU shows readers); only the house labels go. The replacement list and its wording are part of
the round-2 protocol freeze, with a test that no listed label survives into a blinded file.

Every write-up of round 1 states: "Blinding was partial: writing style allowed a model to identify systems, and
WHU's scope notes were left in."
